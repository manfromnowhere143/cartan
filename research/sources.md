## Claims under investigation

| Claim | Status / evidence | Falsification or missing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H1 Coupled uncertainty tracking yields useful recovery beyond competent procedures | Proposed; no CARTAN measurement | Reject an algorithm claim if B0 with the same information/actions achieves the same outcomes; equal-information improvements need a concrete task and cost |
| H2 An additional measurement justifies its system burden | Proposed; S03/S07/S16 establish serious alternatives | Reject if independence is illusory, it cannot distinguish relevant states, or lifecycle cost exceeds benefit |
| H3 There is an economically material operator problem | Unknown; S10 is adverse context | Needs owner requirements, actual procedures, credible fault exposure and consequences; an illustrative case is insufficient |
| H4 Software contract/model checks establish physical safety | Rejected | Data validation and model verification cannot validate the physical assumptions |
| H5 Every apparent ambiguity can be solved by more computation | Rejected under the specified information model | Indistinguishable states with incompatible safe actions require additional information, changed hardware, or conservative refusal |

H5 is a mathematical implication of the proposed model, not an external empirical
finding: if identical observable histories occur for two states, a policy supplied
only that history cannot know which state occurred. Its robust action set is the
intersection of admissible actions across those states. An empty intersection of
progress actions does not imply a hazardous action is justified.

## Coverage and remaining searches

This is a bounded review, not an exhaustive systematic review. CMU methods above
give concrete standards for comparison; Waterloo remains an ambition benchmark.
The bounded Waterloo-specific searches did not recover a suitably matched primary
method for this exact task. This is a search limitation, not a claim that such work
does not exist. Neither institution is affiliated with or endorses CARTAN.

Missing: full Gemini conversation and opportunity reassessment; lawful HOTDOCK
manual and recovery semantics; vendor fault data; complete standards/IP review;
mission-owner requirements; calibrated sensing, loads and energy limits. The
GSFC-STD-8013 landing-page retrieval failed; search metadata alone is not adopted
as a requirements source. Blind-mate means a connection designed for mating without
direct visual alignment; it does not itself establish isolation or retention.
Dead-face and HVIL implementations need circuit-specific documentation, including
residual energy and common-cause failure coverage, before they can be credited.

No source's inaccessible detail is treated as proof of an unserved gap. Historical
Starfish/Artemis/launch-price/ADRAS-J statements were read in the packet but are
not freshly verified premises or current-status claims in this block.
