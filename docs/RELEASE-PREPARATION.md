## License decision

The proposed choice for CARTAN-owned code, original documentation, synthetic
fixtures and original study output is Apache-2.0. Its explicit contributor patent
grant is one reason to consider it for reusable engineering software; that grant
does not clear third-party hardware patents. The license also specifies notice
retention and redistribution terms. [Official Apache text](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)

MIT remains a simpler permissive alternative requiring preservation of copyright
and permission notices. Proprietary distribution remains the alternative if
Daniel does not intend an open-source grant. Neither permissive license establishes
hardware safety or ownership of outside material. [MIT text and conditions](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/mit/)
