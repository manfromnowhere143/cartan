is requested only after intent. Compared with UI 008, the complete static package
adds 26,797 bytes, including UI 009's 678 bytes. The aperture contributes 26,119

click to verified content followed by two animation-frame callbacks. These are

and observed long tasks are in `bounded-profile.json`. `motion-0.png`,
`motion-260.png` and `motion-520.png` inspect paused native effects, not video FPS.
